Cookies

Description

Decadent and chewy, these chocolate cookies offer a deep, indulgent cocoa flavor with a soft, melt-in-your-mouth center and slightly crisp edges. Each bite delivers a perfect balance of sweetness and richness, often enhanced with chunks or chips of semi-sweet or dark chocolate for extra depth. The texture is delightfully tender, with a satisfying density that makes them perfect for pairing with a glass of milk or coffee. Ideal for chocolate lovers, these cookies are comforting, timeless, and irresistibly satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (125g) all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup (45g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup (113g) unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup (100g) granulated sugar
  • ½ cup (100g) brown sugar (packed)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup (175g) chocolate chips or chunks (semi-sweet or dark)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, using a hand or stand mixer, beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together until light and fluffy (about 2to 3 minutes).
  4. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract until fully combined.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredient mixture to the wet mixture, mixing just until combined. Don’t overmix.
  6. Stir in the chocolate chips or chunks with a spatula or spoon.
  7. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough (or use a cookie scoop) onto the prepared baking sheet, spacing about 2 inches apart.
  8. Bake for 10–12 minutes, or until the edges are set but the centers still look soft.
  9. Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
