Lemonade

Description

Lemonade is a refreshing, sweet-tart beverage made primarily from fresh lemon juice, water, and sugar. Popular worldwide, it’s known for its bright citrus flavor and hydrating qualities, especially during warm weather. Simple to prepare and endlessly customizable, it can be served still or sparkling, and flavored with herbs like mint, fruits like berries, or even spices for a twist on the classic.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (240 ml) freshly squeezed lemon juice (about 4–6 lemons)
  • ¾ to 1 cup (150–200g) granulated sugar (to taste)
  • 4–5 cups (1–1.2 L) cold water
  • Ice, for serving
  • Optional: lemon slices and fresh mint for garnish

Steps

  1. In a small saucepan, combine sugar and 1 cup of water. Heat over medium, stirring until the sugar dissolves completely. Let it cool slightly.
  2. In a large pitcher, combine the lemon juice and the cooled syrup. Add the remaining cold water and stir well.
  3. Taste and add more sugar or lemon juice if needed. Chill in the refrigerator or serve over ice.
  4. Pour into glasses filled with ice. Garnish with lemon slices or fresh mint if desired.
