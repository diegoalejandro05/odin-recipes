Pizza

Description

A classic pepperoni pizza featuring a homemade yeast leavened dough, topped with tangy tomato sauce, generous layers of melted mozzarella cheese, and crispy, spicy pepperoni slices. The crust is golden and chewy, with a slightly crisp edge.

Ingredients

  • 3 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 ¼ cups warm water
  • 2 ¼ tsp active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ¾ cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 25 to 30 slices pepperoni
  • oregan

Steps

  1. Make Dough: Activate yeast in warm water with sugar (5 min). Mix with flour, salt, and olive oil. Knead 8 to 10 mins. Let rise ~1 hour.
  2. Preheat Oven: 475°F (245°C) with pizza stone or tray inside.
  3. Assemble Pizza: Roll out dough, spread sauce, add cheese, top with pepperoni.
  4. Bake: 12 to 15 mins until crust is golden and cheese bubbles.
